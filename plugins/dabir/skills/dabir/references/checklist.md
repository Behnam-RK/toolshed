# Final review

Run before offering to publish; fix what fails. Also the brief for momayez
reviewers: each failed item is a finding.

1. From the headline alone, does a busy PM know what changes, why now, and
   what is asked of them?
2. Do the headings alone tell the story?
3. Is the body free of code names, paths, branches and flags?
4. Does every P# land in a D#, an explicit deferral, or Out of scope?
5. Does every Open decision have its options with costs, a recommendation or
   the missing evidence, an owner, and a decide-by (or "unknown")?
6. Does every risk state what, who, lasting or not, and mitigation? Does
   every unfinished item carry a `Missing:` line?
7. Is every claim read from a source, told by the user, or labelled an
   assumption? No invented dates, numbers or owners?
8. Can an engineer find what they need in the appendices or a linked source?
9. For each body sentence: can it move to an appendix, or go? Move or cut
   every one that can.
10. Revisions: does Appendix A answer every comment thread on vN-1?
