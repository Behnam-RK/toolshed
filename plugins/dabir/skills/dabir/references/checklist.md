# Final review

Run before offering to publish; fix what fails. Also the brief for momayez
reviewers: each failed item is a finding.

1. Read only the headline: does a busy PM know the change, why it is urgent
   and what they must do?
2. Read only the headings: does the story hold together?
3. Above the divider, is there any identifier a PM would not recognise
   (class, path, branch, flag)?
4. Solution's Problem table: does every P# row point to a D#, "deferred:
   why" or "out of scope: why"?
5. Each Open decision: options with their costs, a recommendation or the
   evidence still missing, a named owner, and a decide-by date or "unknown"?
6. Risks table: is any cell empty? Solution: does anything built but not
   released lack a `Missing:` line?
7. Is every claim read from a source, told by the user, or labelled an
   assumption? No invented dates, numbers or owners?
8. Would an engineer find the mechanism, links and history they need in the
   appendices?
9. For each body sentence: can it move to an appendix, or go? Move or cut
   every one that can.
10. Revise mode only: does Appendix A answer every comment thread on vN-1?
